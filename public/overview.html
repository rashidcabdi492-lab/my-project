POLARIS FOOTBALL CLUB

Kisii Teaching and Referral Hospital (KTRH) — Doctors & Community Football Club

ℹ️ Club Overview

⚽ Reasons for Formation

  • Physical Fitness & Wellness: Providing healthcare personnel and guest players an avenue for regular exercise.
  • Mental Health & Stress Relief: Offering a recreational outlet to burn off workplace stress and prevent burnout.
  • Teamwork & Community: Building strong bonds between KTRH medical personnel and the surrounding community.
  • Competitive Participation: Representing KTRH in corporate leagues, regional medical tournaments, and open games.

📋 Rules of Polaris FC

  1. Inclusive Membership: Open to KTRH staff, medical interns, and guest community players.
  2. Punctuality & Attendance: All members must adhere to training schedules and match timings.
  3. Sportsmanship & Respect: Zero tolerance for abusive language or aggressive conduct toward anyone.
  4. Integrity: Conduct on and off the pitch must maintain high sports and professional standards.
  5. Financial Dues: Mandatory member contributions cover pitch rentals, balls, kits, and league fees.

🤝 How to Join

  • Step 1 (Select Form): Choose the KTRH Staff form or Non-KTRH Guest form below.
  • Step 2 (Submit Details): Provide your contact details, position, and work/location info.
  • Step 3 (Training Evaluation): Attend scheduled weekly practice sessions to integrate into the squad.
  • Step 4 (Registration): Complete official player registration and remit membership dues.
